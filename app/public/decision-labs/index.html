05b — Our mission

Strategic intelligence shouldn’t be reserved for the largest organizations.

High-quality research and expert decision support have been hard for smaller organisations to reach. Qubeterra combines AI-powered workflows with human expertise to make them more accessible, scalable and flexible. AI-augmented consulting extends human judgement, it does not replace it.

Large corporation

  • Internal research teams
  • Consulting budgets
  • Cross-department decisions

Growing SME

  • A smaller team
  • Limited resources
  • Equally important decisions
